# Supply Chain Performance Analysis

Statistical analysis of a supply chain dataset (100 orders) completed during
my field training at Jeddah Chamber of Commerce.

## Objectives
- Relationship between shipping cost and revenue (linear regression)
- Revenue differences by transport mode (one-way ANOVA)
- Effect of location on shipping time (one-way ANOVA)
- Independence of inspection results from supplier (chi-square)

## Tools
Excel (Data Analysis, MegaStat), Power BI

## Key Findings
- Shipping cost, transport mode, and location showed no significant effect
  on revenue or delivery time.
- Inspection results depend significantly on the supplier (p = 0.003).
